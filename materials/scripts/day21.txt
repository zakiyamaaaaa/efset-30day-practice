The survey suggests that customers like the new self-service machines, but remember who answered it: people who had successfully used a machine and then completed the screen survey. We heard very little from those who gave up and went to the staffed counter. Before removing any counters, we should talk to that group as well. I'm not arguing against the machines. I just don't want a decision about everyone's access to be based only on the experiences of people who already find the technology easy.
