Your gym membership lets you attend most classes without paying extra. The exception is the small-group swimming course, which has a separate fee because places are limited to four people. You can cancel a class booking free of charge until six hours before it starts. After that, a fee applies unless the instructor cancels the class. Please note that simply failing to arrive counts as a late cancellation. If you are unsure you'll make it, remove your booking early so another member can use the place.
