Before we order new desks, I'd like us to borrow a few adjustable ones for a month. They look useful, but the staff who tried them at the exhibition only used them for a few minutes. A longer trial would show whether people actually change position during a normal working day. We should include colleagues with different roles, not just those who already want a standing desk. Otherwise, we may get enthusiastic feedback that doesn't represent the whole office. Purchasing can wait until we have that information.
