Sales increased after we changed the product packaging, which is encouraging. But I wouldn't attribute the entire rise to the new design. We also reduced the price that month, and a popular blogger mentioned the product. Customers in our survey liked the clearer label, yet that doesn't tell us why they bought it. Before spending money on redesigning the whole range, I'd test the new packaging on one more product while keeping its price unchanged. That should give us a clearer comparison.
