We tried sending all customer questions to one shared inbox. This made it easier to see what had arrived, but it created another problem: several people sometimes answered the same message. To prevent that, we've added a simple rule. Whoever starts handling a question puts their name beside it. They don't have to solve it immediately, but they become responsible for keeping the customer updated. We'll review the system next week; there's no need to buy new software unless this change fails to help.
