The online induction has reduced the time managers spend repeating basic information, but new staff say they miss the chance to ask small questions informally. I don't think returning to a full day of presentations is the answer. We could keep the online modules and add a short conversation with a colleague during the first week. That person wouldn't replace the manager or assess performance; they'd simply explain everyday practices. Let's try it with the next group of recruits and ask which questions still go unanswered before deciding whether to expand it.
