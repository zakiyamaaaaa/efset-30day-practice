At first I thought the repair service was too expensive. A new kettle costs only a little more, after all. Then the technician explained that the fee includes checking the cable, replacing the damaged switch, and a six-month guarantee. That changed my view, especially because I like the kettle I already own. I still wouldn't repair every cheap appliance, but in this case the price seems reasonable. Next time, I'll ask what's included before comparing repair costs with the price of a replacement.
