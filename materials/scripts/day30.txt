The town's tool-lending service is popular, but the waiting list is growing for a few items, especially drills. Buying more of everything would be expensive and might leave less-used tools sitting on shelves. I'd first shorten the loan period for the busiest items, provided members can request an extension when no one is waiting. We should also record unsuccessful requests, since completed loans alone don't show unmet demand. After a month, we'll be better placed to decide which extra tools are worth buying and whether the shorter loans create difficulties.
