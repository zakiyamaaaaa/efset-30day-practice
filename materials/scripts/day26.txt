The four-day-week trial worked well in our design team, but we shouldn't assume the same arrangement will suit customer support. Designers can often move tasks between days, whereas the support line must remain open throughout the week. That doesn't rule out shorter weeks for support staff; it may mean rotating days off instead of closing on Fridays. Before making a company-wide announcement, I'd ask each team to propose a workable schedule and explain how service levels would be maintained. The principle might transfer even if the exact timetable doesn't.
